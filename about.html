Hello, I'm Durojaiye Rofiat Titilayo

I am a Cybersecurity Analyst with a professional foundation in Security Operations and an interest in how technology, security and business come together.

My professional story

My work in cybersecurity has shaped the way I think about digital security: beyond alerts and technical controls, security is also about understanding risk, protecting business operations, supporting informed decisions and building resilience.

I am particularly interested in the practical side of cybersecurity: understanding what happens, why it happens, how to investigate it and what can be done to strengthen defenses.

My experience in Security Operations

Security Operations is my current professional foundation. It is where I practise the day-to-day discipline of monitoring, investigating and understanding security events.

What cybersecurity means to me

For me, cybersecurity should not exist in isolation from the business it protects. Effective security requires both technical understanding and an appreciation of people, processes, priorities and risk.

Why I am exploring Cloud Security

I have recently started exploring Cloud Security. I want to develop a stronger understanding of how cloud environments are secured, and how organisations can build secure and scalable systems. This is an area I am learning, and I document my progress on the Learning Journey page.

My interest in Identity and Access Management

I am also exploring Identity and Access Management (IAM): how identities and permissions are managed, and why getting access right matters for both security and operations. Follow my notes on the Learning Journey page.

Cybersecurity and Business: the bigger picture

My exposure to business and operational processes has strengthened my curiosity about how security can support efficiency, governance, risk management and organisational objectives. It is a perspective I bring to technology, and one I continue to develop.

Read more in Insights.

My interest in AI Security

I am interested in the evolving relationship between cybersecurity, cloud technologies, identity, artificial intelligence and business strategy. AI Security is an area of interest for me rather than a specialisation.

My approach to learning and problem-solving

I am continuously learning, documenting practical projects and expanding my technical perspective. My approach is to ask what happened, why it happened, how to investigate it, and what would strengthen defences, and to separate evidence from assumptions.

Values and professional principles

  • Evidence first. Distinguish what was observed from what was assumed.
  • Security with context. Protect the business it serves.
  • Honest about growth. Label learning as learning.
  • Share knowledge. Document work so others can learn from it.

Current focus and future direction

While Security Operations is my current professional foundation, I am building depth in Cloud Security and IAM, and continuing to connect technical work with business resilience and decision-making. This portfolio is a growing record of my professional experience, practical work, learning and ideas.
